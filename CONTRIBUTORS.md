# Contributors and acknowledgements

Gozar thanks the contributors who make private and censorship-resistant communication possible.

## Guardian Project and Orbot

Special thanks to **The Guardian Project**, **Nathan Freitas**, and all **Orbot** contributors.

Gozar's Android Tor integration uses the official Tor-Android `TorService` and `TorControlConnection` APIs and adapts the connection, binding, shutdown, reconfiguration, and binding-death lifecycle from Orbot.

- Orbot: https://github.com/guardianproject/orbot-android
- Guardian Project: https://guardianproject.info/
- Tor-Android: https://github.com/guardianproject/tor-android
- Orbot license: BSD 3-Clause; see `ORBOT-LICENSE.txt` and `NOTICE`.

Orbot is not required at runtime. The Guardian Project and Orbot contributors do not endorse Gozar.
